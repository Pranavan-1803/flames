How Does the Love Calculator Work?

The True Love Calculator is a fun, algorithmic tool designed to measure the hypothetical romantic compatibility between two people based purely on their names. While real love takes time, trust, and communication, there's always a thrill in seeing a high percentage flash on the screen when you type in your crush's name!

Our Love Calculator uses a deterministic name-matching algorithm. This means if you type the exact same two names, you will always get the exact same percentage. It analyzes the numerical value of the letters in both names, computes the overlapping vocal harmonies, and generates a score from 0% to 100%.

Understanding Your Score

  • 0% - 20%: Consider yourselves acquaintances. Getting past the friend zone might require a miracle!
  • 21% - 40%: There might be a slight spark, but it will take a lot of work to turn this into a roaring fire.
  • 41% - 60%: A solid middle ground. You likely share some interests and could make it work with effort.
  • 61% - 80%: Great compatibility! You naturally click and probably enjoy spending a lot of time together.
  • 81% - 100%: Soulmates! A near-perfect match. If this is your crush, you might want to ask them out right now!

Is it Scientifically Accurate?

Of course not! Name calculators are meant strictly for entertainment. While there is a psychological phenomenon called the "Name-Letter Effect" where people tend to prefer things (and sometimes other people) that share letters with their own name, it is not a basis for true romantic compatibility.

Don't let a low score stop you from pursuing a relationship, and don't let a 100% score convince you to marry someone you just met. Use this calculator as a fun icebreaker!
