The Science of Name Numerology

Numerology is an ancient esoteric science based on the principle that the universe is a system, and once broken down we are left with the basic elements: numbers. Your Name Numerology (or Destiny Number) is calculated by converting the letters of your full birth name into numbers and reducing them down to a single digit.

Our completely free calculator uses the popular Pythagorean numerology method. In this system, each letter of the alphabet is assigned a number from 1 to 9 based on its place in the alphabet (A=1, B=2, C=3, etc.). By adding these numbers together until you get a single digit, the calculator reveals the core vibrations and personality traits associated with your specific name.
