The Magic of the Crush Probability Oracle

Ever found yourself staring at your phone, agonizing over whether your crush is going to text you back or not? The Crush Probability Oracle is a fun digital toy designed to take the edge off the waiting game.

Our algorithm takes the name you enter, runs it through a highly unscientific process of randomized magic strings, and spits out a probability score. While it absolutely cannot predict the actual future (because free will exists!), it is a fantastic tool for sharing a laugh with your friends while you wait for that notification ping.
