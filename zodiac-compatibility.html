Astrology and Love: Are You Compatible?

Astrological compatibility checks the elemental and planetary interactions between two sun signs. While a full astrological synastry chart (comparing a person's sun, moon, rising, Venus, and Mars placements) gives the most accurate reading, our calculator provides a fun, generalized score based strictly on sun sign harmony!

The twelve zodiac signs are divided into four core elements: Fire, Earth, Air, and Water. Signs of the same element generally get along seamlessly because they share core values and ways of perceiving the world. Complementary elements (like Earth and Water, or Fire and Air) also spark intense, balancing connections.
